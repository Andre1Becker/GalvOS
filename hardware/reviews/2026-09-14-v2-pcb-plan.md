# V2 PCB implementation plan

Goal: a separate schematic-matched v2 PCB using approved external 12 V fan supplies and 12.6 V buck input. Preserve the legacy PCB and user-owned untracked `agents.md`. Use explicit staging and intermediate versioned pushes; draft checkpoints are not production releases.

## Approved architecture

J2 supplies the buck at 12.6 V; the buck supplies 5 V electronics. J4/J6 supply J5/J7 respectively at external 12 V. Both positive fan rails are separate from each other and the buck positive rails, with common power ground. New two-layer board: provisional 175 x 115 mm outline. Preserve inverted RGB, independent DAC/SD SPI buses and safety functions. No unrelated circuit/firmware changes or Gerbers.
