## Placement and copper

R12/R17 feedback resistors were rotated/repositioned near U12; C8 was moved and reoriented toward its supply pin. R7/R14/R6/R16 were reoriented/repositioned for feedback/output access. Existing footprints and all electrical net memberships were retained.

The routed PCB has 964 segments and 103 vias. Its first ground regions are:

- B.Cu AGND: x=2–94, y=12–64 mm.
- B.Cu power ground: x=124–173, y=2–113 mm.
- F.Cu local buck thermal ground: x=142.1–147.5, y=36.5–43.3 mm.

Zone clearance is 0.20 mm, minimum thickness 0.25 mm, thermal gap 0.30 mm and spoke width 0.40 mm. Floating islands are removed. Buck pad 9 uses solid copper connection for its exposed pad and thermal holes. The two ground nets remain separate except through the existing R26 component.

The TI LMR33630 datasheet, section 10, recommends short/wide input/bootstrap/bypass connections, local feedback, a soldered exposed pad, thermal vias and top/bottom ground copper. These initial planes implement part of that guidance, not a completed thermal/EMI design. The manufacturer also recommends multilayer ground shielding and sufficient copper weight; the approved two-layer design still requires explicit load/temperature/EMC qualification.

Source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr33630.pdf

## Router and verification safeguards

Freerouting 2.4.1 was downloaded from its official release, used locally with analytics/API server disabled and a temporary user-data directory. No router dependency or user configuration was added to the repository.

- JAR SHA-256: `251101c3eeac22d7e7dfcf6796603279e5d1000283eb82d8f093780f7afc6aa9`.
- Source: https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/releases/tag/v2.4.1
- DSN SMD-to-SMD clearance was corrected from the exporter's 0.05 mm override to 0.20 mm before routing.
- Existing copper was protected. Fan/buck main positive nets used a provisional 0.8 mm class, now retained as `PowerDraft` in the project. No load rating follows from that width.
- The router still generated 69 neckdowns below the project's 0.20 mm minimum; widening them produced no native clearance errors.
- R9 needed a manually placed 0.60/0.30 mm escape via and a second routing pass. Native connectivity then reached zero unrouted items.
- Native save/export operations can reset project settings. The old v2.0.2 project had 0.00 mm silk clearance despite its documentation; v2.0.3 corrects it to 0.10 mm and adds a guard for minima, skipped checks and unexpected findings.
- Zone generation is verified by inspecting saved zone count, net assignments and fill state as well as native DRC. A zero-unconnected report alone does not prove that a requested plane exists.
